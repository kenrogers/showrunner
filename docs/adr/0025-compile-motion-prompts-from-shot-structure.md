# Compile Motion Prompts from Shot structure

Showrunner will treat the executable text sent with a Take request as a Motion Prompt, not as the full continuity bible.

References carry identity, composition, lighting, wardrobe, and style. The Motion Prompt focuses on positive, direct subject motion, optional scene motion, camera motion, style descriptors, and timing for one short Shot.

This follows the Runway Gen-4 guidance: start from a high-quality input image, keep text simple, describe what should happen, avoid negative phrasing, and avoid re-describing the image in detail.
