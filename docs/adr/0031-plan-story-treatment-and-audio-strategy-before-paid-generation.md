# Plan story treatment and audio strategy before paid generation

## Decision

Showrunner will create a first-class Production Process, Story Treatment, and Audio Strategy as part of the Film Package before paid References, Takes, speech, music, Assembly, Export, or Final Review.

The Production Process is selected first from the kind of video being made: short film, music video, trailer, marketing video, explainer, documentary, social clip, or other. It defines the production goal, required creative decisions, required assets, shot design rules, audio plan, and review criteria for that kind. The Story Treatment then fills in the creative content inside that process.

For short-film briefs, the default Audio Strategy should prefer sparse dialogue plus music over wall-to-wall narration. Narration remains first-class for explainers, trailers, product teasers, and user-requested voiceover. Music is required when the Audio Strategy says it is required, and the Sound Mix must preserve Music Cues instead of dropping them during artifact rendering.

## Consequences

- Director planning must classify the video kind and choose a kind-specific Production Process before clip generation.
- Director planning must decide protagonist, goal, obstacle, stakes, ending, grounding rules, style rules, and audio mode before clip generation.
- Marketing videos must lock audience, offer, proof, product visibility, and CTA; music videos must lock music/rhythm/motif; trailers must lock hook/escalation/final sting; short films must lock character/story causality.
- Motion prompts should describe concrete physical actions, practical settings, props, blocking, camera motion, and consequences, not abstract AI symbolism by itself.
- Scriptwriting can improve dialogue and narration, but should not invent continuous narrator lines for dialogue-led or music-led treatments.
- Final Review cannot pass while required dialogue, narration, or music assets are missing.
- Sound Mix rendering must combine Selected Take audio, dialogue, narration, and music with ducking rules.
